# Suffixe — what German suffixes roughly mean

> The companion card to [Präfixe](Präfixe.md). The division of labour is
> simple: **a prefix changes the meaning, a suffix changes the category**.
> A suffix is the word’s passport — it states the part of speech, the
> gender, the plural, and which layer of the lexicon the word comes from.

## What a suffix tells you

| It fixes | Example | What you know at once |
|---|---|---|
| part of speech | *frei → die Freiheit* | it is a noun |
| gender | *-ung, -heit, -schaft* | always **die** |
| plural | *die Wohnung → -en* | the ending is predictable |
| declension | *der Journalist* | weak (n-declension) |
| register and origin | *-ieren, -tion, -ität* | a loanword, stressed suffix |

Hence the practical payoff: German gender mostly **need not be memorised**
— the tail of the word gives it away.

## 1. Nouns: gender follows the suffix

### Feminine — the most reliable group

| Suffix | Sense | Examples | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| *-ung* | process or result, from a verb | *die Wohnung, die Übung* | *-en* |
| *-heit* | quality, from an adjective | *die Freiheit, die Schönheit* | *-en* |
| *-keit* | same, after *-ig, -lich, -bar, -sam* | *die Möglichkeit* | *-en* |
| *-schaft* | collective or state (Eng. *-ship*) | *die Freundschaft* | *-en* |
| *-ei* | place of work; pejorative collective | *die Bäckerei* | *-en* |
| *-in* | female person | *die Lehrerin, die Ärztin* | *-innen* |
| *-t / -st* | old deverbal noun | *die Fahrt, die Sicht, die Kunst* | varies |
| *-e* | quality, often with umlaut | *die Größe, die Länge, die Kälte* | *-n* |
| *-ion, -tät, -ik, -ur, -ie, -age* | loanwords | *die Nation, die Kultur* | *-en* |

⚠️ Loan suffixes are **always stressed**: *Natio**n**, Universit**ä**t,
Biolog**ie**, Gar**a**ge* — which also tells you how to pronounce the word.

Note the English parallels: *-schaft* ↔ *-ship* (*Freundschaft* ↔
*friendship*), *-heit* ↔ *-hood* / *-ness*, *-ung* ↔ *-ing* / *-tion*.

### Masculine

| Suffix | Sense | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| *-er* | agent or instrument (Eng. *-er*) | *der Lehrer, der Wecker* |
| *-ler, -ner* | same, after certain stems | *der Sportler, der Rentner* |
| *-ling* | a person, tinged small or dependent | *der Lehrling, der Feigling* |
| *-ismus* | doctrine, movement | *der Kapitalismus* (pl. *-ismen*) |
| *-ist, -ent, -ant* | person, loan → **weak declension** | *der Journalist**en*** |
| *-eur, -or, -är* | person, loan | *der Ingenieur, der Doktor* |

### Neuter

| Suffix | Sense | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| *-chen, -lein* | diminutive (+ umlaut) | *das Brötchen, das Bächlein* |
| *-um* | loanword | *das Zentrum* (pl. *Zentren*) |
| *-ment* | loanword | *das Dokument, das Instrument* |
| *-tum* | state, domain | *das Eigentum, das Wachstum* |
| *-nis* | result (das **or** die) | *das Ergebnis*, but *die Erlaubnis* |
| infinitive as a noun | the activity itself | *das Essen, das Leben, das Lesen* |

⚠️ Three classic traps: *das Mädchen* is neuter because **the suffix
outranks the sex**; *der Reichtum* and *der Irrtum* are masculine despite
*-tum*; and *-nis* wavers between *das* and *die*.

## 2. Adjectives

| Suffix | Sense | Examples | English kin |
|---|---|---|---|
| *-ig* | having the property | *sonnig, hungrig, ruhig* | *-y*: *sunny* |
| *-lich* | pertaining to; softening | *freundlich, rötlich, täglich* | *-ly* |
| *-isch* | belonging to; often evaluative | *praktisch, russisch, kindisch* | *-ish* |
| *-bar* | can be done to (passive) | *essbar, sichtbar, machbar* | *-able* |
| *-sam* | inclined to | *sparsam, langsam, aufmerksam* | *-some* |
| *-haft* | in the manner of | *fehlerhaft, meisterhaft* | — |
| *-los* | without | *arbeitslos, hilflos* | *-less* |
| *-voll* | full of | *wertvoll, liebevoll* | *-ful* |
| *-mäßig, -gemäß* | in accordance with | *ordnungsgemäß, planmäßig* | — |
| *-frei* | free of (positively) | *alkoholfrei, zuckerfrei* | *-free* |
| *-reich, -arm* | rich in / low in | *kinderreich, fettarm* | *-rich* |
| *-wert, -würdig* | worth, worthy of | *sehenswert, glaubwürdig* | *-worthy* |

**Three pairs worth keeping apart:**

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| *kindlich* / *kindisch* | childlike (warm) / childish (a reproach) |
| *geistlich* / *geistig* | spiritual, clerical / mental, intellectual |
| *sinnlich* / *sinnig* | sensual / sensible, apt |

The same *-ly* / *-ish* split exists in English (*childlike* vs *childish*),
which makes this pair easy to internalise.

**The *-los* / *-frei* pair:** both mean “without”, but *-frei* says the
absence is **good news** (*alkoholfrei, bleifrei, stressfrei*), while *-los*
is neutral or **bad news** (*arbeitslos, obdachlos, sinnlos*).

⚠️ *-bar* does not mean “bearing” but “-able”: *tragbar* — portable,
*unbezahlbar* — priceless. English borrowed *-able* from Latin, but German
*-bar* is native, from *tragen* “to carry” — the same root as English *bear*.

## 3. Verbs

| Suffix | Sense | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| *-eln* | diminutive, repeated action | *lächeln, tröpfeln, streicheln* |
| *-ern* | repeated movement | *rudern, klettern, flattern* |
| *-igen* | make something such | *reinigen, beruhigen, beschleunigen* |
| *-ieren* | loan verbs, stress on *-íeren* | *studieren, diskutieren* |
| *-isieren* | turn into, bring into a form | *modernisieren, realisieren* |

⚠️ Verbs in *-ieren* take **no *ge-*** in the participle: *studiert*,
*telefoniert*. 📎 [Partizip II](Partizip_II.md).

## 4. Adverbs

| Suffix | Sense | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| *-weise* | manner, in portions | *schrittweise, teilweise* |
| *-erweise* | comment on the whole statement | *glücklicherweise, dummerweise* |
| *-wärts* | direction (Eng. *-ward*) | *vorwärts, rückwärts, heimwärts* |
| *-halber* | for the sake of | *sicherheitshalber, umständehalber* |
| *-maßen* | to such a degree | *einigermaßen, bekanntermaßen* |

The *-weise* / *-erweise* difference is subtle but real:

> *Er hat teilweise recht.* — He is partly right. (**how** — manner)
> *Glücklicherweise hat er recht.* — Fortunately he is right. (a comment)

English has the same two jobs in one ending: *he answered stupidly* vs
*stupidly, he answered* — German keeps them formally apart.

## 5. What a suffix tells you instantly

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| *-ung* | die, pl. *-en*, built from a verb |
| *-heit / -keit* | die, pl. *-en*, built from an adjective |
| *-chen / -lein* | das, plural unchanged, umlaut in the stem |
| *-ist, -ent, -ant* | der, **weak declension**: *den Studenten* |
| *-tion, -tät, -ie* | die, stress on the suffix, pl. *-en* |
| *-bar, -sam, -ig, -lich* | it is an adjective |
| *-ieren* | a loan verb, participle without *ge-* |

## 6. A closer look at *-er*

The same tail does three unrelated jobs; context tells them apart:

| Role | Example | Note |
|---|---|---|
| agent / instrument | *der Fahrer, der Toaster* | der, plural unchanged |
| inhabitant of a city | *der Berliner* | also a doughnut 🍩 |
| indeclinable attribute | *die Berliner Mauer* | capitalised, never inflected |
| comparative | *schneller, größer* | not a noun suffix at all |

## How to remember

**A prefix answers “what are we doing”, a suffix answers “what did it
become”.** The prefix at the front pulls the meaning sideways; the suffix at
the back stamps the word: part of speech, gender, declension.

Three anchors cover most of the gender problem:

- **The feminine trio:** *die Wohn**ung**, die Frei**heit**, die
  Freund**schaft*** — process, quality, community. Plus every loanword in
  *-tion, -tät, -ie*.
- **The neuter pair:** *das Mäd**chen** mit dem Bröt**chen*** — a diminutive
  is neuter whatever the stem means.
- **The masculine agent:** *der Lehr**er**, der Fahr**er**, der Weck**er***
  — whoever, or whatever, performs the action.

One test phrase: *Die Freiheit des Lehrers und das Mädchen mit dem
Brötchen.* — three suffixes, three genders, no exceptions.
