# verkündigen — to proclaim, to herald

A high-register verb meaning **to announce solemnly**, **to
proclaim**, **to preach**. You meet it above all in religious, legal
and ceremonial contexts.

## Grammar

| Feature    | Value                                        |
|------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Type       | weak, inseparable                            |
| Forms      | *verkündigt — verkündigte — hat verkündigt*  |
| Government | *jemandem* (dat.) *etwas* (acc.) verkündigen |

## Main meanings

### 1. To proclaim, to preach (religious)

- *das Evangelium verkündigen* — to preach the Gospel
- *die frohe Botschaft verkündigen* — to proclaim the glad tidings
- *Gottes Wort verkündigen* — to preach the word of God

> *Der Engel verkündigte Maria die Geburt Christi.*
> The angel announced the birth of Christ to Mary.
> *(hence the feast of **Mariä Verkündigung** — the Annunciation)*

### 2. To proclaim, to declare formally

- *ein Urteil verkündigen* — to pronounce a verdict
- *ein Gesetz verkündigen* — to promulgate a law
- *die Unabhängigkeit verkündigen* — to declare independence

> *Das Gericht verkündigte das Urteil.*
> The court pronounced the verdict.

## verkündigen vs. verkünden

These are two forms of **one verb** with nearly identical meaning,
separated by a fine stylistic line:

| Form            | Register                     | Where it is used             |
|-----------------|------------------------------|------------------------------|
| **verkünden**   | a shade less solemn          | news, laws, decisions        |
| **verkündigen** | more solemn, archaic         | religion, preaching, classics|

Compare:

- *Der Sprecher verkündete das Wahlergebnis.* ✅ neutral
  *(The spokesperson announced the election result.)*
- *Der Prophet verkündigte das Kommen des Herrn.* ✅ elevated
  *(The prophet proclaimed the coming of the Lord.)*

In the modern press and in ordinary speech you will meet
**verkünden** far more often. **Verkündigen** is already a step
towards the pulpit or literary pathos — roughly the distance between
English *announce* and *proclaim / herald*.

## Word breakdown

> **ver-** + **kündigen**

- **ver-** here is intensifying: “fully, out loud, to the end”
- **kündigen** comes from **Kunde** in its old sense “tidings, news”
  (compare *kund tun* — to make known)

That old *Kunde* is the same word as English **-kith** in *kith and
kin* and, more usefully, as the *-know-* in **to make known**. The
whole verb is “to make known with full force”.

⚠️ Modern **kündigen** (to give notice, to terminate a contract) is a
**false friend**! It grows from the same root — literally “to make
known that one is leaving” — but the meaning has narrowed to the
employment office.

| Word                        | Meaning                                    |
|-----------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| **kündigen**                | to give notice, terminate a contract       |
| **verkünden / verkündigen** | to proclaim, announce solemnly             |
| **ankündigen**              | to announce in advance, to herald an event |
| **bekunden**                | to express, to declare (feelings, views)   |
| **die Kunde** (elevated)    | tidings, news                              |

## Derived nouns

- **die Verkündigung** — proclamation, annunciation, pronouncement
  - *Mariä Verkündigung* — the Annunciation
  - *die Urteilsverkündung* — the pronouncement of a verdict
    *(usually with -kündung)*
  - *die Gesetzesverkündung* — the promulgation of a law
- **der Verkündiger** — herald, preacher *(bookish)*

## Register

| Register             | Use                                               |
|----------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| ⛪ religion          | *das Evangelium verkündigen* — its living home    |
| ⚖️ law               | *ein Urteil verkündigen / verkünden*              |
| 🏛️ politics, history | *die Republik verkündigen / verkünden*            |
| 📰 neutral press     | prefers **verkünden**                             |
| ❌ everyday speech   | not used (there: *sagen, mitteilen, ankündigen*)  |

## How to remember

The root **Kunde** means “tidings” (compare modern *Kundschaft* —
customers, *Erdkunde* — geography, literally “earth-knowledge”).
**Ver-kündigen** = “to put out the tidings with full force, to
proclaim them to the world”.

If someone *verkündigt* something, they didn’t just say it — they
**heralded** it. Picture a prophet on a mountain, a judge in a
courtroom, an angel before Mary. Anything smaller than that is plain
*sagen* or *mitteilen*.
