# die Verpflegung — catering, provisions, board

A feminine noun meaning **the provision of food** — especially in an
organised setting: on a trip, at a hotel, in the army, at an event.
Neutral but faintly administrative in tone.

## Main meanings

### 1. Board, catering (as a service or process)

- *Vollverpflegung* — full board (all meals included)
- *Halbpension* / *Halbverpflegung* — half board (breakfast + dinner)
- *Verpflegung an Bord* — catering on board
- *Übernachtung mit Verpflegung* — overnight stay with meals included

> *Im Preis sind Unterkunft und Verpflegung inbegriffen.*
> Accommodation and board are included in the price.

### 2. Provisions, rations (the food itself)

- *Verpflegung für die Wanderung einpacken* — to pack provisions for
  the hike
- *die Truppen mit Verpflegung versorgen* — to supply the troops with
  rations

> *Wir haben Verpflegung für drei Tage dabei.*
> We have provisions for three days with us.

## Grammar

| Feature | Value |
|----------|----------|
| Gender | **die** (feminine) |
| Plural | practically never used |
| With prepositions | *mit Verpflegung*, *ohne Verpflegung* |

## Where the word comes from

From the verb **verpflegen** “to feed, to cater for (in an organised
way)”. The root is old **pflegen** in its original sense “to look
after, to tend, to care for” (compare *die Pflege* — care, nursing).

So **Verpflegung** is literally “**organised care in the matter of
food**”. The prefix **ver-** here carries the sense “fully, see it
through” (as in *versorgen* — to supply, provide for).

English cognate note: *pflegen* is related to English **to plight**
(as in *plight one’s troth*) — the shared idea being to pledge oneself
to look after something. The modern senses have drifted far apart, so
treat it as a family resemblance, not a translation.

## Verpflegung vs. Essen vs. Nahrung

- **Essen** — food in general, everyday word (*Das Essen ist fertig* —
  dinner’s ready)
- **Nahrung** — food as a biological category (*gesunde Nahrung* —
  wholesome nutrition)
- **Lebensmittel** — groceries, foodstuffs (what you buy in a shop)
- **Verpflegung** — food **as an organised provision** (on a trip, at
  a hotel, in the forces, at an event)

English splits the same way: *food* vs *nutrition* vs *groceries* vs
**catering / board / rations**.

## Typical contexts

- 🏨 tourism: *Hotel mit Verpflegung* — hotel with board
- ✈️ transport: *Verpflegung im Flugzeug* — inflight catering
- 🎖️ military: *Truppenverpflegung* — troop rations
- 🏫 institutions: *Schulverpflegung*, *Kantinenverpflegung*
- 🎪 events: *Verpflegung der Gäste* — catering for the guests

## How to remember

**Pflege** is care — the word on nursing-home signs. **Verpflegung**
is care delivered through food: someone is looking after your
stomach. If a hotel listing says *Verpflegung inklusive*, your meals
are being taken care of — the catering, not the cooking.
