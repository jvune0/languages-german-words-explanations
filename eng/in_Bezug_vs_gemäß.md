# in Bezug auf vs. gemäß — “with regard to” vs. “in accordance with”

> Two staples of written officialese that learners routinely swap, because
> English *regarding* and *according to* feel close. They are not: *in Bezug
> auf* names the **topic** (“what this is about”), while *gemäß* names the
> **norm** the action conforms to (“what it complies with”). Both are 📰⚖️
> written and formal; speech has lighter alternatives.

## Grammar: the whole difference in one table

| Feature | *in Bezug auf* | *gemäß* |
|---|---|---|
| Case | **accusative** (governed by *auf*) | **dative** |
| Position | before the noun only | before **or after** the noun |
| What it introduces | a topic, a subject matter | a norm, rule, source |
| English equivalent | with regard to, as regards | in accordance with, pursuant to |
| Syntax | prepositional phrase | a single preposition |
| Register | 📰 business prose, a bit heavy | ⚖️ legal, administrative |

> *in Bezug auf **den** Vertrag* — acc. (as regards the contract)
> *gemäß **dem** Vertrag* = ***dem** Vertrag gemäß* — dat. (per the contract)

⚠️ **Spelling.** Since the 1996 reform only *in **B**ezug auf* with a capital
*B* is correct; the old *in bezug auf* is obsolete.

## 1. *in Bezug auf* (+ acc.) — “with regard to, as regards”

The phrase **flags the topic**: a statement about that topic follows. It
claims nothing about conformity.

> *In Bezug auf die Kosten haben wir noch keine Entscheidung getroffen.*
> With regard to the costs we have not yet reached a decision.

> *Was ändert sich in Bezug auf die Datensicherheit?*
> What changes as regards data security?

> *In Bezug auf Ihre Anfrage vom 3. März teilen wir Ihnen mit …*
> Further to your enquiry of 3 March we wish to inform you …

Variants of the same construction:

| Variant | Case | Flavour |
|---|---|---|
| *mit Bezug auf* | acc. | slightly more formal, business letters |
| *unter Bezugnahme auf* | acc. | 📜 officialese of the highest order |
| *Bezug nehmen auf* | acc. | the verbal form: *Ich nehme Bezug auf …* |
| *bezüglich* | **gen.** | short and dry: *bezüglich Ihrer Anfrage* |

## 2. *gemäß* (+ dative) — “in accordance with, pursuant to”

This one asserts that **the action conforms to a norm**. What follows must
be something conformable: a law, a section, a contract, an instruction, a
plan, an order, a wish.

> *Gemäß § 5 des Vertrages ist die Kündigung schriftlich zu erklären.*
> Pursuant to § 5 of the contract, termination must be declared in writing.

> *Die Anlage wurde gemäß den Vorschriften gewartet.*
> The plant was serviced in accordance with the regulations.

> *Wir haben ganz seinen Wünschen gemäß gehandelt.*
> We acted entirely in accordance with his wishes.

Postposition (*dem Vertrag gemäß*) sounds a shade more literary and stresses
the conformity itself; legal texts prefer the preposed order.

**The living suffix *-gemäß*.** The same idea attaches to nouns:

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *ordnungsgemäß* | duly, properly, by the book |
| *vorschriftsgemäß* | as prescribed, per regulations |
| *vertragsgemäß* | in accordance with the contract |
| *pflichtgemäß* | as duty requires |
| *altersgemäß* | appropriate to one’s age |

## The key: *Bezug* is a thread, *gemäß* is a ruler

- ***Bezug*** comes from *beziehen* (*ziehen* “to pull” + *be-*): “to draw
  a connection to something”. Hence *die Beziehung* (relationship) and
  *sich beziehen auf* (to refer to). English gives the exact parallel:
  *relate to* ← Latin *re-latus* “carried back”, and *with reference to*
  ← *referre* “to carry back”. *In Bezug auf X* = “drawing a thread to X”.
- ***gemäß*** comes from ***das Maß*** “measure” (plus the associative
  prefix *ge-*): “measured against, commensurate with”. Relatives:
  *mäßig*, *angemessen*, *ermessen*, and in English *measure*, *mete*,
  and the archaic adjective *meet* “fitting” (*it is meet*). *Gemäß X* =
  “cut to the measure of X”.

> **The thread (*Bezug*) shows what you are talking about. The measure
> (*Maß*) shows what it complies with.**

## How not to confuse them: the substitution test

| If English wants… | Then German wants |
|---|---|
| “as regards…”, “with respect to…” | *in Bezug auf* |
| “in accordance with…”, “pursuant to…”, “under” (a law) | *gemäß* |

> ❌ *Gemäß der neuen Situation müssen wir umplanen.*
> A situation is not a norm; nothing can comply with it. Use instead:
> ✅ *In Bezug auf die neue Situation …* or *Angesichts der neuen Situation …*

> ❌ *In Bezug auf § 5 ist zu kündigen.* (reads as “on the topic of § 5”)
> ✅ *Gemäß § 5 ist zu kündigen.* — Termination is required under § 5.

One question settles it: **“about what?” → *in Bezug auf*; “by what?” →
*gemäß*.**

## Neighbours worth keeping apart

### Close to *in Bezug auf* (“about, concerning”)

| Phrase | Case | Register and nuance |
|---|---|---|
| *bezüglich* | gen. | 📰 dry and businesslike; letters and forms |
| *hinsichtlich* | gen. | 📰 “in respect of”, used in analysis |
| *im Hinblick auf* | acc. | 📰 “in view of, with an eye to” — forward-looking |
| *was … betrifft / angeht* | — | 💬 the living idiom: *Was den Preis angeht, …* |
| *in puncto* | — | 💬 colloquial, faintly ironic: *in puncto Pünktlichkeit* |
| *betreffs* | gen. | 📜 archaic officialese |
| *zu* | dat. | plain and short: *Fragen zum Vertrag* |

### Close to *gemäß* (“according to”)

| Preposition | Case | When exactly |
|---|---|---|
| *gemäß* | dat. | a norm: law, contract, regulation, instruction ⚖️ |
| *entsprechend* | dat. | “correspondingly” — softer, about general fit |
| *laut* | gen./dat. | the **source of a statement**: *laut Zeugenaussage* 📰 |
| *nach* | dat. | the neutral default: *nach dem Gesetz*, *meiner Meinung nach* |
| *zufolge* | dat., usually postposed | *Berichten zufolge* — “reportedly” 📰 |
| *aufgrund* | gen. | a **cause**, not a norm: *aufgrund des Regens* |
| *im Sinne von* | — | “within the meaning of”: *im Sinne des Gesetzes* |

The *gemäß* / *laut* split is everywhere in the news:

> *Laut Polizei gab es drei Verletzte.* — According to the police, three
> people were injured. (source of information)
> *Die Festnahme erfolgte gemäß § 127 StPO.* — The arrest was made under
> § 127 of the Code of Criminal Procedure. (legal basis)

## Careful: *Bezug* has other meanings too

| Meaning | Example |
|---|---|
| connection, relevance | *ein Bezug zur Praxis* — a link to practice |
| cover, slipcase | *der Bettbezug* — duvet cover |
| receipt, subscription | *der Bezug einer Zeitung*, *Bezug von Sozialhilfe* |
| *die Bezüge* (pl.) | salary, emoluments of an official |

## Typical contexts

- **Business letters:** *In Bezug auf Ihr Schreiben vom …*; *Unter
  Bezugnahme auf unser Telefonat …*
- **Legal texts:** *gemäß Artikel 5 Absatz 2*, *gemäß den geltenden
  Bestimmungen*, *ordnungsgemäße Zustellung*.
- **Reports and analysis:** *In Bezug auf die Vorjahreszahlen ist der Umsatz
  gestiegen.*
- **Manuals:** *Das Gerät ist gemäß Bedienungsanleitung zu betreiben.*
- **Speech:** neither one — say *Was den Vertrag angeht, …* and *Nach dem
  Gesetz darf man das nicht.*

## How to remember

Picture an office desk with **a thread** and **a ruler** on it.

- **Bezug** is the **thread** you pull (*ziehen*) from your text to a
  subject: it only shows **what** you are about to discuss. Compare English
  *with reference to*, *relate to*.
- **gemäß** is the **ruler**, *das Maß*: you hold the action up against the
  norm and check whether it **measures up**. Compare English *measure*,
  *mete*, and archaic *meet* “fitting”.

One sentence with both: *In Bezug auf Ihre Anfrage teilen wir mit, dass die
Kündigung gemäß § 5 erfolgt ist.* — “With regard to your enquiry, we inform
you that termination was effected pursuant to § 5.” First the thread to the
topic, then the ruler against the law.
