# feststellen vs. hervorheben — “to establish” vs. “to highlight”

> The two verbs turn up in the same kinds of text — reports, minutes,
> academic papers — and English often renders both as “note”. They do
> different jobs, though: *feststellen* answers **“what is actually the
> case”**, *hervorheben* answers **“what matters most here”**. One pins
> down a fact, the other sets the emphasis.

## Grammar

| Feature | *feststellen* | *hervorheben* |
|---|---|---|
| Prefix | *fest-*, separable | *hervor-*, separable |
| Type | weak: *stellte fest – festgestellt* | strong: *hob hervor – hervorgehoben* |
| Auxiliary | *haben* | *haben* |
| Government | *etw.* (acc.); *dass*-clause | *etw.* (acc.); *dass*-clause |
| Reflexive | ❌ | ✅ *sich hervorheben von* + dat. |
| Needs a background | no | **yes**: you highlight against something |
| Implies importance | no, it is neutral | yes, that is its whole point |

> *Der Arzt stellte eine Lungenentzündung fest.* — The doctor diagnosed
> pneumonia.
> *Der Redner hob die Rolle der Freiwilligen hervor.* — The speaker
> highlighted the role of the volunteers.

## The key: a nail versus a lever

Both verbs describe a physical act, and that act explains the figurative
sense exactly.

- ***feststellen*** = *fest* (firmly, immovably; English **fast** as in
  *hold fast*) + *stellen* (to place). Literally “**to set it fast**”, to
  lock it in place. The fact is placed so that it can no longer move or be
  disputed. 📎 The whole family: [fest-Verben](fest-Verben.md).
- ***hervorheben*** = *her* (hither) + *vor* (forward) + *heben* (to lift;
  English **heave**). Literally “**to heave it forth**” — to lift one item
  out of a row and put it where it can be seen. English *highlight* is built
  on the same idea: raise it into the light.

> **feststellen nails a fact down; hervorheben lifts one point above the
> rest. A nail against a lever.**

## 1. *feststellen* — to establish, ascertain, note, discover

A neutral, minute-taking verb: it evaluates nothing, it only records. Three
typical uses:

> *Wir stellen fest, dass die Frist abgelaufen ist.*
> We note that the deadline has expired. (the formula of minutes)

> *Ich musste feststellen, dass mein Fahrrad weg war.*
> I discovered that my bike was gone. (a personal discovery)

> *Es lässt sich nicht mehr feststellen, wer den Fehler gemacht hat.*
> It can no longer be established who made the mistake. (an inquiry)

The technical sense survives literally: *die Feststellbremse* (parking
brake), *die Feststelltaste* (Caps Lock — the key that locks the case).

## 2. *hervorheben* — to highlight, emphasise, single out

### 2.1. Emphasis in speech or writing

> *Sie hob hervor, wie wichtig die Zusammenarbeit gewesen sei.*
> She emphasised how important the cooperation had been.

> *Besonders hervorzuheben ist die Qualität der Übersetzung.*
> The quality of the translation deserves particular mention.

> *Der Bericht hebt die Fortschritte hervor, verschweigt aber die Kosten.*
> The report highlights the progress but says nothing about the cost.

The phrase ***besonders hervorzuheben ist …*** is the standard formula of
reviews, references and academic surveys.

### 2.2. Visual highlighting

> *Wichtige Begriffe sind im Text fett hervorgehoben.*
> Key terms are highlighted in bold in the text.

The participle *hervorgehoben* is the ordinary typographic “highlighted”.

### 2.3. *sich hervorheben* — to stand out against

> *Die roten Dächer heben sich deutlich von der Landschaft hervor.*
> The red roofs stand out clearly against the landscape.

## Summary table

| Aspect | *feststellen* | *hervorheben* |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | records a fact | sets an emphasis |
| The question | “is this so?” | “what is the main thing here?” |
| Speaker’s stance | neutral | evaluative |
| Needs context | no | yes, a background |
| Typical adverbs | *nüchtern, sachlich, amtlich* | *besonders, ausdrücklich, lobend* |
| Can be visual | ❌ | ✅ (bold, colour) |
| Register | 📰⚖️ minutes, medicine, law | 📰🎓 reviews, talks, research |

## How not to confuse them

A quick test: try the English word first.

| If you mean… | Then use |
|---|---|
| “it was established that…”, “it turned out” | *feststellen* |
| “to single out”, “to give pride of place” | *hervorheben* |

> ❌ *Der Arzt hob eine Lungenentzündung hervor.* — reads as though the
> doctor picked pneumonia out as the most interesting of the diseases.
> ✅ *Der Arzt stellte eine Lungenentzündung fest.*

> ❌ *Ich möchte feststellen, wie gut Ihre Arbeit war.* — a dry record where
> praise is wanted. ✅ *Ich möchte hervorheben, wie gut Ihre Arbeit war.*

Because their jobs differ, the two go together very naturally:

> *Wir stellen fest, dass die Ziele erreicht wurden, und heben besonders
> den Beitrag des Teams hervor.*
> We note that the targets were met, and particularly highlight the team’s
> contribution.

## Neighbours

### Around *feststellen*

| Verb | Flavour |
|---|---|
| *konstatieren* | a bookish Latinism for the same thing 📜 |
| *festhalten* | to record in writing: *im Protokoll festhalten* |
| *bemerken* | to notice, to remark (in passing, unofficial) |
| *ermitteln* | to establish by investigation ⚖️ |
| *diagnostizieren* | to diagnose (medical) |
| *sich herausstellen* | ⚠️ **to turn out**: *Es stellte sich heraus, dass …* |

### Around *hervorheben*

| Verb | Flavour |
|---|---|
| *betonen* | the everyday “to stress”; emphasis in speech 💬📰 |
| *unterstreichen* | to underline; figuratively, to reinforce |
| *herausstellen* | to bring to the fore (more active, more showy) |
| *akzentuieren* | 📜 bookish, stylistic |
| *herausstreichen* | to play up, to talk up (often disapproving) |
| *hervorbringen* | ⚠️ **to produce, to bring forth**, not to highlight |
| *hervorgehen aus* | ⚠️ **to follow from**: *Daraus geht hervor, dass …* |

⚠️ *betonen* vs *hervorheben*: *betonen* is simply saying something with
stress and fits any conversation; *hervorheben* always implies **a set to
be singled out from**, and reads as more written.

📎 A verb of a different class from the same minutes: *beschließen*
describes nothing, it **creates** a new state of affairs — it resolves.
See [beschließen vs. entscheiden](beschließen_vs_entscheiden.md).

## Typical contexts

- **Minutes and law:** *Wir halten fest / stellen fest, dass …*; *die
  Identität feststellen*.
- **Medicine and engineering:** *einen Defekt / eine Krankheit feststellen*.
- **Reviews and references:** *Besonders hervorzuheben ist …*;
  *hervorragende Leistungen hervorheben*.
- **Research:** *Die Studie hebt hervor, dass …*
- **Typesetting:** *farblich hervorgehoben*, *fett hervorgehoben*.

## How to remember

Two pictures, both taken straight from the literal verbs:

- **feststellen** — **a hammer and nail**: the fact is nailed down *fest*
  (English *hold fast*) so that it can no longer shift. No evaluation, just
  “this is how it is”.
- **hervorheben** — **a lever**: one item is lifted (*heben*, English
  *heave*) and pushed forward (*hervor*) above the others — exactly what
  English *highlight* does by raising it into the light.

One sentence with both: *Wir **stellen fest**, dass alles funktioniert, und
**heben** die Arbeit des Teams besonders **hervor**.* — “We note that
everything works, and particularly highlight the team’s work.” First nail
the fact down, then lift the main thing up.
