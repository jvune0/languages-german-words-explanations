# fest- (feststellen · festhalten · festnehmen …) — “fast, firmly, for good”

> One separable prefix, one idea — *make it so it can no longer move* —
> spread across “ascertain”, “hold on”, “arrest” and “moor”. Mostly
> neutral vocabulary, with a distinctly officialese/police branch.

## Grammar: what the whole family shares

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Prefix type | separable (*trennbar*), always stressed: *FESTstellen* |
| Source | the adjective *fest* “firm, solid, tight” |
| Separation | *Ich stelle fest, dass …* / *Die Polizei nahm ihn fest.* |
| Past participle | *fest-ge-stellt*, *fest-ge-halten*, *fest-ge-nommen* |
| Infinitive + *zu* | *festzustellen*, *festzuhalten*, *festzunehmen* |
| Auxiliary | virtually always *haben* |
| Base verb | keeps its own conjugation: *nehmen → nahm → genommen* |

**Spelling trap.** If *fest* is intensified or comparative, it reverts to
an ordinary adverb and is written separately:

> *Halt das Seil ganz fest!* — Hold the rope really tight!
> *Er hat den Knoten fester gezogen.* — He pulled the knot tighter.
> But: *Halt dich fest!* — Hold on! (the verb *sich festhalten*)

## The master key: German *fest* = English *fast*

German *fest* and English **fast** are the same word (OHG *festi*, OE
*fæst*). The original sense was never “quick” but “**immovable, stuck**”
— exactly what survives in *hold fast*, *stuck fast*, *steadfast*, *make
fast*, *fasten*, *shut fast*. “Quick” came later, via “running hard”.
So the formula is:

> **fest + verb = “do the verb so that the thing can no longer move”**

What stops moving may be an object (*festbinden* — tie down), a person
(*festnehmen* — arrest), or a **fact** (*feststellen* — establish). The
abstract senses are just the rope and the nail applied to ideas.

⚠️ Beware: German *fast* means “**almost**” (*fast fertig* — almost
done), while German *fest* means “fast/firm”. The pair is a perfect
trap for English speakers.

## The main verbs

### 1. *feststellen* — to establish, ascertain, note, detect

Government: *etw.* (acc.) *feststellen*; very often with a *dass*-clause
or an indirect question. Core image: to *stellen* (put, place) a fact
down *fest* (firmly), so that it stops being in dispute.

> *Der Arzt stellte eine Lungenentzündung fest.*
> The doctor diagnosed pneumonia.

> *Ich musste feststellen, dass mein Fahrrad weg war.*
> I had to note (discover) that my bike was gone.

> *Es lässt sich nicht mehr feststellen, wer den Fehler gemacht hat.*
> It can no longer be established who made the mistake.

The literal technical sense is alive and well: *feststellen* = “to lock
in place”. Hence *die Feststellbremse* (parking brake) and *die
Feststelltaste* (the Caps Lock key — the “lock it down” key).

> *Bitte stellen Sie die Bremse fest.* — Please engage the brake.

### 2. *festhalten* — to hold tight; to record; to stick to

Four constructions worth learning as a block:

| Construction | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *jdn./etw. festhalten* | hold on to, detain | *Halt den Hund fest!* |
| *sich festhalten (an + dat.)* | hold on (to sth.) | *Halt dich am Geländer fest!* |
| *an etw.* (dat.) *festhalten* | stick to, adhere to | *am Plan festhalten* |
| *etw. festhalten* (written) | record, capture | *im Protokoll festhalten* |

> *Zwei Polizisten hielten ihn fest, bis der Krankenwagen kam.*
> Two police officers held on to him until the ambulance arrived.

> *Halt dich fest: Wir haben gewonnen!*
> Brace yourself — we won! (idiomatic lead-in to big news)

> *Sie hält an ihrer Meinung fest, obwohl alle widersprechen.*
> She sticks to her opinion even though everyone disagrees.

> *Der Fotograf hat den Moment für immer festgehalten.*
> The photographer captured the moment forever.

Note how well English tracks German here: *hold fast* → *hold on to*,
and “put it on record” is exactly *festhalten* in minutes and contracts:
*Wir halten fest, dass …* — “Let the record show that …”.

### 3. *festnehmen* — to arrest, take into custody

Government: *jdn.* (acc.) *festnehmen*. People only, and legal only:
to *nehmen* (take) someone so that they cannot leave. English **“take
into custody”** carries the same picture; *custody* itself is a
holding word.

> *Der Verdächtige wurde am Bahnhof festgenommen.*
> The suspect was arrested at the station.

> *Die Polizei nahm drei Männer vorläufig fest.*
> Police provisionally detained three men.

The noun *die Festnahme* (“arrest, apprehension”) is everyday news
German.

### 4. *festlegen* — to lay down, fix, determine

Government: *etw. festlegen*; *sich auf etw.* (acc.) *festlegen* — “to
commit oneself, pin oneself down”.

> *Wir müssen den Termin endlich festlegen.*
> We finally have to fix the date.

> *Das Gesetz legt fest, wer haftet.* — The law lays down who is liable.

> *Ich will mich noch nicht festlegen.* — I don’t want to commit yet.

The English match is word-for-word: *legen* “to lay” → **lay down** the
rules.

### 5. *festsetzen* — to set, fix (a price, a date); to detain

> *Der Preis wurde auf 200 Euro festgesetzt.*
> The price was set at 200 euros.

> *Man setzte ihn im Gefängnis fest.* — He was confined in prison.
> (officialese; *festnehmen* is the everyday word)

> *Der Staub setzt sich in den Ritzen fest.*
> The dust lodges itself in the cracks.

*sich festsetzen* = “to lodge, settle in for good” — dust, people, or an
idea in someone’s head (*Der Gedanke setzte sich in ihr fest*).

### 6. *festmachen* — to fasten; to moor; to settle (a date)

> *Wir haben das Boot am Steg festgemacht.*
> We made the boat fast to the jetty.

The nautical sense is literally English **“to make fast”**. Colloquially
it means to nail something down:

> *Können wir einen Termin festmachen?* — Can we pin down a date?

And one argumentative use worth having: *etw. an etw.* (dat.)
*festmachen* — “to base a claim on something”:

> *Woran machst du das fest?* — What are you basing that on?

### 7. *feststehen* — to be settled, be certain

Intransitive, usually impersonal: *es steht fest, dass …*.

> *Es steht fest, dass er zurücktritt.* — It is certain that he will
> resign.

> *Der Termin steht noch nicht fest.* — The date isn’t settled yet.

### 8. The rest of the physical series

| Verb | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *festbinden* | tie down | *das Pferd am Baum festbinden* |
| *festschrauben* | screw on tight | *das Regal an der Wand festschrauben* |
| *festkleben* | glue on, stick fast | *Der Kaugummi klebt fest.* |
| *festdrücken* | press down firmly | *das Pflaster festdrücken* |
| *festfrieren* | freeze in place | *Das Boot ist festgefroren.* |
| *sich festfahren* | get stuck, deadlock | *Wir haben uns festgefahren.* |
| *sich festbeißen* | latch on to (a topic) | *Er hat sich festgebissen.* |

## Summary: what gets pinned down

| Verb | Object | What happens | Register |
|---|---|---|---|
| *feststellen* | a fact, a defect | it gets put on record | neutral/formal |
| *festhalten* | person, object, idea | it isn’t let go of | neutral |
| *festnehmen* | people only | liberty is removed | legal |
| *festlegen* | rule, date | it is settled by decision | formal/neutral |
| *festsetzen* | sum, date, person | fixed by authority | officialese |
| *festmachen* | boat, part, meeting | physically fastened | neutral/colloq. |
| *feststehen* | a fact (subject!) | already decided | neutral |

## Distinguishing near neighbours

### “Establish / notice”

| Word | Nuance |
|---|---|
| *feststellen* | note, ascertain; final, on-the-record tone |
| *bemerken* | notice in passing; also “to remark” |
| *merken* | sense, become aware (*Ich merke, dass …*) |
| *erkennen* | recognise, identify, see the truth of |
| *ermitteln* | determine by investigation or calculation |
| *herausfinden* | find out, dig up (more colloquial) |

> *Ich habe gemerkt, dass etwas nicht stimmt* — I sensed it.
> *Ich habe festgestellt, dass etwas fehlt* — I established it.

### “Arrest / detain”

| Word | Nuance |
|---|---|
| *festnehmen* | to arrest, take into custody (the standard word) |
| *verhaften* | to arrest, typically on a warrant; weightier |
| *festsetzen* | to confine, hold (officialese) |
| *inhaftieren* | to imprison, take into detention (legal term) |
| *aufhalten* | ⚠️ to hold up, delay — not to arrest |

⚠️ *Der Zug wurde aufgehalten* — the train was delayed.
*Der Dieb wurde festgenommen* — the thief was arrested.

### “Fix / determine”

| Word | Nuance |
|---|---|
| *festlegen* | lay down so it stops changing |
| *festsetzen* | impose from above (price, fine, deadline) |
| *bestimmen* | determine, decree; also “to destine for” |
| *vereinbaren* | agree on — requires a second party |

## Related words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *fest* (adj.) | firm, solid, tight, permanent |
| *die Festigkeit* | firmness, strength (of materials) |
| *befestigen* | to fasten, attach, fortify |
| *die Feststellung* | finding, observation, statement |
| *die Festnahme* | arrest |
| *die Festlegung* | fixing, stipulation |
| *festangestellt* | permanently employed, on staff |
| *der Festpreis* | fixed price |
| *die Festplatte* | hard disk (“the firm platter”) |
| *das Festnetz* | landline network |
| *fest schlafen* | to sleep soundly |

⚠️ **False relative:** *das Fest* “festival, celebration”
(*Weihnachtsfest*, *Festessen*, *festlich*) comes from Latin *festum*
and has nothing to do with *fest* “firm” — just as English *feast* and
*fast (firm)* are unrelated. *Festplatte* is “hard”, *Festessen* is
“festive”.

## Typical contexts and collocations

- **Medicine and engineering:** *eine Krankheit / einen Defekt / einen
  Mangel feststellen*.
- **Bureaucracy:** *die Identität feststellen* (to establish someone’s
  identity), *etw. amtlich feststellen lassen*.
- **Minutes and negotiation:** *Wir halten fest, dass …* — “Let’s put
  on record that …”.
- **News:** *Drei Tatverdächtige wurden festgenommen.*
- **Scheduling:** *einen Termin festlegen / festmachen*.
- **Small talk:** *Halt dich fest!* — “You’d better sit down for this.”

## How to remember

Picture a **nail**. *fest* is English *fast* as in *hold fast* — driven
in so nothing shifts. Then just add the base verb:

- *stellen* “put” + fast → **put a fact down for good** = ascertain;
- *halten* “hold” + fast → **hold and don’t release** = hold on, record,
  stick to;
- *nehmen* “take” + fast → **take and don’t release** = arrest;
- *legen* “lay” + fast → **lay down** a rule;
- *machen* “make” + fast → **make fast** a boat, nail down a date;
- *stehen* “stand” + fast → **it stands firm** = it’s settled.

One sentence holds the whole family: *Die Polizei nahm ihn fest, hielt
ihn fest und stellte fest, wer er war.* — “The police arrested him, held
him, and established who he was.” Three verbs, one nail.
