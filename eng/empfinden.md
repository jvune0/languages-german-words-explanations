# empfinden — to feel (inwardly); to perceive something as

A strong verb with an inseparable prefix. The register is faintly
literary: where everyday German says *fühlen*, **empfinden** is more
precise and more inward. Its core image is "**to find within**" — a
feeling is not something that falls on you, but something you
**discover inside yourself**.

## Grammar

| Feature | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Type | strong; prefix **inseparable**, unstressed |
| Principal parts | *empfindet · empfand · hat empfunden* |
| Participle II | *empfunden* — **no *ge-*** |
| Subjunctive II | *empfände* (📚 alive and frequent) |
| *zu*-infinitive | *zu empfinden* (never *empzufinden*) |
| No present umlaut | *du empfindest, er empfindet* |
| Government | *etw.* (acc.) *empfinden* |
| Key pattern | *etw. **als** etw. empfinden* — to perceive as |
| Also | *etw. **für** jdn. empfinden* — to feel for someone |
| Auxiliary | *haben* |

The conjugation mirrors *finden*: *fand – gefunden* → *empfand –
empfunden*. The only difference is the missing *ge-*.

⚠️ **No reflexive use.** *Ich empfinde mich gut* is impossible; for
how you feel, German has only *sich fühlen* — *ich fühle mich gut*.

## Main meanings

### 1. To feel, to experience (an emotion, pain)

The direct object is the feeling itself: *Freude*, *Schmerz*, *Reue*,
*Mitleid*, *Erleichterung*, *Hass*.

> *Ich empfand große Erleichterung.*
> I felt great relief.

> *Er empfand keinerlei Reue.*
> He felt no remorse whatsoever.

> *Beim Einstich empfindet man kaum Schmerz.*
> You hardly feel any pain when the needle goes in.

### 2. *etw. als etw. empfinden* — to perceive sth. as sth.

The most frequent and most useful pattern: "subjectively, this is how
it strikes me". Standard in argument and in psychology.

> *Er empfand die Bemerkung als Beleidigung.*
> He took the remark as an insult.

> *Viele empfinden die Regel als ungerecht.*
> Many perceive the rule as unfair.

> *Ich empfinde das als Zumutung.*
> I find that an imposition.

### 3. *etw. für jdn. empfinden* — to have feelings for someone

In romantic contexts this is almost always the verb of choice.

> *Sie empfindet nichts mehr für ihn.*
> She no longer feels anything for him.

> *Was empfindest du eigentlich für mich?*
> What do you actually feel for me?

> *Er empfand tiefe Zuneigung für seine Schülerin.*
> He felt a deep affection for his student.

### 4. To feel physically (of the body, the environment) — rarer

> *Man empfindet die Kälte hier stärker als in der Stadt.*
> The cold is felt more keenly here than in town.

> *gefühlte / empfundene Temperatur* — the perceived temperature

## Word breakdown and etymology

**emp-** (= *ent-*) + **finden**

- **finden** — "to find" = English **find**. A sensation is imagined
  as a **discovery**: something you come upon inside yourself.
- **emp-** is the phonetic variant of the prefix **ent-**, from
  Germanic ***and-*** "against, towards" — the same element as in
  **Ant**wort (= English **answer**). The archaic "towards", not the
  modern "un-, de-". For the sound change *ent-f- → emp-f-*, see
  [emp-Verben](emp-Verben.md).

Literally: **"to find towards"** — to take in what comes at you and
find it inside.

Old High German *intfindan* meant something quite concrete, "to grope
out, to come upon"; the abstract "to feel" grew out of that later.

**English parallel.** *Find* preserves the very same bridge into
perception: *I find it insulting* is a word-for-word analogue of *ich
empfinde es als Beleidigung*. English **find + adjective** simply *is*
the *empfinden als* construction, which makes this pattern one of the
easiest in German to internalise — you already have it.

## How not to confuse it

| Verb | Core idea | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **empfinden** | to feel inwardly; more literary, precise | *Reue empfinden* |
| **fühlen** | to feel — the general word | *Ich fühle nichts* |
| **sich fühlen** | to feel (a state of health/mood) | *Ich fühle mich gut* |
| **spüren** | to sense in the body, notice physically | *Kälte spüren* |
| **merken** | to notice, to realise (mentally) | *Ich merke es* |
| **wahrnehmen** | to perceive (neutral, near-technical) | *Reize wahrnehmen* |
| **finden** | to find, to think (a light opinion) | *Ich finde es gut* |
| **halten für** | to consider sth. to be (a judgement) | *für falsch halten* |

**The key splits:**

- **empfinden vs. fühlen.** *Fühlen* is universal and colloquial;
  *empfinden* is a shade more formal and points to a **conscious
  inner experience**. Both work in love, but *Was empfindest du für
  mich?* runs deeper than *Was fühlst du?*
- **empfinden vs. spüren.** *Spüren* is about **the body and an
  outside signal** (*Ich spüre den Wind*); *empfinden* is about the
  **processed experience** (*Ich empfinde Angst*).
- **empfinden als vs. finden / halten für.** All three deliver a
  verdict, but *finden* is a light opinion (*Ich finde es gut*),
  *halten für* a reasoned judgement (*Ich halte es für falsch*), and
  *empfinden als* an **emotional reading**, often about hurt feelings.

## Watch out: empfindlich vs. empfindsam

Two adjectives from one verb, and a permanent source of confusion:

| Word | Sense | Colouring |
|------|-------|-----------|
| **empfindlich** | easily hurt; fragile; keenly felt | ⚠️ often negative |
| **empfindsam** | tender-hearted, sentimental | 📚 literary |
| **feinfühlig** | sensitive **to others**, tactful | ✅ positive |

The *empfindliche* person **suffers**, the *empfindsame* one **feels
for others**. English *sensitive* covers both — see the card on
[empfindlich](empfindlich.md).

## Register and style

| Setting | Usage |
|---------|-------|
| 📚 Literature, essays | ✅ its natural habitat |
| 🧠 Psychology, philosophy | ✅ near-technical (*Schmerz empfinden*) |
| 📰 Press | ✅ especially *als … empfunden werden* |
| ⚖️ Legal language | ✅ (*als unzumutbar empfunden*) |
| 💬 Conversation | ➖ everyday speech takes *fühlen*, *finden* |
| ❌ How you feel | ❌ only *sich fühlen*, never *empfinden* |

## Typical collocations

- *Freude / Schmerz / Reue / Mitleid / Angst empfinden*
- *tiefe Zuneigung / Dankbarkeit / Hass empfinden*
- *etw. als ungerecht / peinlich / befreiend empfinden*
- *als störend empfunden werden* — to be perceived as disruptive
- *nichts mehr für jdn. empfinden* — to feel nothing for sb. any more
- *meinem Empfinden nach* — to my mind, as I perceive it
- *ein feines Empfinden für etw. haben* — to have a fine instinct for
- *gefühlte / empfundene Temperatur* — the perceived temperature

## How to remember

Split it: **emp- ("towards") + finden ("find")** = **"to find
towards"**. A feeling does not arrive ready-made from outside — you
**find** it in yourself when something comes your way.

The English bridge is literal: *I **find** it insulting* = *ich
**empfinde** es als Beleidigung*. Same verb, same construction.

And keep the three *emp-* preterites side by side: *empfing* — he
**caught** (a guest, a signal); *empfahl* — he **advised**; *empfand*
— he **felt**. One prefix, three roots: *fangen*, *felhan*, *finden*.
