# -stellen (vorstellen · einstellen · herstellen · bestellen …) — “to put upright”

> *stellen* means “to stand something up”, and the prefix says **where**:
> forward, in, out, under, instead. But the family has a second and more
> important logic: most of its senses are “to put something **into a
> position**” — into a queue, into a job, on display, into question. That is
> where “hire”, “introduce”, “manufacture” and “pretend” all come from.

## Grammar of the base verb

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | **weak**: *stellen – stellte – gestellt* |
| Auxiliary | *haben* |
| Government | *etw.* (acc.) + where to: *in / auf / an / unter* + **acc.** |
| Reflexive | *sich stellen* — to stand; to turn oneself in; to feign |
| Participle | *ge-stell-t*; inseparables take no *ge-*: *bestellt* |

### The *stellen* / *stehen* pair — and the whole quartet

This is the core of German positional grammar: transitive weak verbs on the
left (“where to?”, acc.), intransitive strong verbs on the right (“where?”,
dat.).

| Action (acc., where to?) | State (dat., where?) |
|---|---|
| *stellen – stellte – gestellt* (put upright) | *stehen – stand – gestanden* (stand) |
| *legen – legte – gelegt* (lay) | *liegen – lag – gelegen* (lie) |
| *setzen – setzte – gesetzt* (set, seat) | *sitzen – saß – gesessen* (sit) |
| *hängen – hängte – gehängt* (hang sth.) | *hängen – hing – gehangen* (hang) |

> *Ich stelle die Flasche **auf den** Tisch.* — I put the bottle on the
> table. (acc.)
> *Die Flasche steht **auf dem** Tisch.* — The bottle is on the table. (dat.)

## The key: the root \*stel- “to set”

*stellen* shares its root with *stehen*, *die Stelle* (place, post), *der
Stall* (stall), *still* (motionless), *die Stellung* (position). The English
kin is direct: **stall**, **still**, **install**, **stallion**. The same
Indo-European root gave Greek *stellein* “to set, to send” — whence
*apostle* (literally “one sent out”) and *epistle*.

> **prefix + stellen = “put it into the position the prefix points to” —
> in space, in society, or in the mind.**

## The main verbs

### 1. *vorstellen* — to introduce; to imagine

The most frequent member of the family, and the family’s prize trap: **the
case of the reflexive pronoun changes the meaning**.

| Construction | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *jdn. vorstellen* | to introduce someone | *Darf ich Ihnen Herrn Weber vorstellen?* |
| *sich* (**acc.**) *vorstellen* | to introduce oneself | *Ich stelle **mich** vor.* |
| *sich* (**dat.**) *etw. vorstellen* | to imagine | *Ich stelle **mir** das vor.* |

> *Stell dir vor, was passiert ist!* — Imagine what happened!
> *Er hat sich bei der Firma vorgestellt.* — He had an interview there.

The difference shows only in the 1st and 2nd person singular (*mich/mir*,
*dich/dir*) — which is exactly where learners slip. Literally: “put yourself
forward” = introduce yourself; “put it before your own eyes” = imagine.

*die Vorstellung* means a performance, an idea, and a job interview
(*das Vorstellungsgespräch*) all at once.

### 2. *einstellen* — to hire; to adjust; to discontinue

| Sense | Example |
|---|---|
| to take on staff | *Die Firma stellt zehn Leute ein.* |
| to set, to adjust | *die Temperatur einstellen* |
| to cease, to discontinue | *Die Produktion wurde eingestellt.* |
| *sich auf etw.* (acc.) *einstellen* | *Stell dich auf Regen ein.* — Expect rain. |

The shared image is “put it inside” — into the payroll, into the right
setting, and in the third sense “put it away for good”.

*die Einstellung* likewise has three senses: hiring, a setting, and an
**attitude** (*eine positive Einstellung zum Leben*).

### 3. *bestellen* — to order; to summon; to appoint; to till

> *Ich habe einen Kaffee bestellt.* — I ordered a coffee.
> *Der Chef hat mich zu sich bestellt.* — The boss summoned me.
> *Er wurde zum Vorsitzenden bestellt.* — He was appointed chairman.
> *Bestell ihr schöne Grüße!* — Give her my regards!
> *das Feld bestellen* — 📜 to till the field (the oldest sense).

### 4. *herstellen* — to manufacture; to establish (a connection)

> *Das Unternehmen stellt Batterien her.* — The company makes batteries.
> *Wir müssen eine Verbindung herstellen.* — We need to establish a link.

*die Herstellung* — production; *der Hersteller* — manufacturer.

### 5. *darstellen* — to depict; to constitute

> *Das Bild stellt eine Schlacht dar.* — The painting depicts a battle.
> *Das stellt ein ernstes Problem dar.* — That constitutes a serious
> problem. (a staple of academic and business prose)
> *Er stellte den Hamlet dar.* — He played Hamlet.

### 6. *anstellen* — to employ; to switch on; to queue; to get up to

| Sense | Example |
|---|---|
| to employ | *fest angestellt sein* — to be on the permanent staff |
| to switch on (colloq.) | *Stell mal das Radio an.* |
| *sich anstellen* — to queue up | *Wir mussten uns lange anstellen.* |
| *sich anstellen* — 💬 to make a fuss | *Stell dich nicht so an!* |
| to get up to something | *Was hast du angestellt?* |

*der/die Angestellte* — a salaried employee (declined like an adjective).

### 7. *abstellen* — to set down; to park; to switch off; to remedy

> *Stell die Tasche kurz ab.* — Put the bag down for a moment.
> *Wo hast du das Auto abgestellt?* — Where did you park the car?
> *Das Wasser wurde abgestellt.* — The water has been cut off.
> *Diesen Missstand müssen wir abstellen.* — We must put a stop to this.

### 8. *aufstellen* — to set up; to nominate; to set (a record)

> *Wir stellen das Zelt auf.* — We’re putting up the tent.
> *Die Partei stellt einen Kandidaten auf.* — The party is fielding a
> candidate.
> *Sie hat einen neuen Rekord aufgestellt.* — She set a new record.
> *eine These aufstellen* — to advance a thesis.

### 9. *ausstellen* — to exhibit; to issue (a document)

> *Das Museum stellt Picasso aus.* — The museum is exhibiting Picasso.
> *Können Sie mir eine Rechnung ausstellen?* — Could you issue me an invoice?

*die Ausstellung* — exhibition; *der Aussteller* — exhibitor; issuer.

### 10. *umstellen* — a two-way verb

| Form | Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| *ÚMstellen* | separable | to rearrange; to switch to | *auf Ökostrom umstellen* |
| *umSTÉLLEN* | inseparable | to surround | *Die Polizei umstellte das Haus.* |

> *Wir haben die Möbel umgestellt.* — We rearranged the furniture.
> *Ich muss mich erst umstellen.* — I need to adjust first.

📎 The stress rule for such pairs: the [Präfixe](Präfixe.md) card.

### 11. *unterstellen* — also two-way

| Form | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *ÚNTERstellen* | to put under shelter | *das Rad unterstellen* |
| *unterSTÉLLEN* | to impute, to insinuate | *Das will ich Ihnen nicht unterstellen.* |
| *jdm. unterstellt sein* | to report to someone | *Er ist mir direkt unterstellt.* |

### 12. *verstellen* / *sich verstellen* — to adjust; to feign

> *Man kann den Sitz verstellen.* — The seat is adjustable.
> *Der Baum verstellt die Sicht.* — The tree blocks the view.
> *Er verstellte seine Stimme.* — He disguised his voice.

### 13. The official layer

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *erstellen* | to draw up, to produce (a document) | *ein Gutachten erstellen* |
| *feststellen* | to establish, to ascertain | 📎 [fest-Verben](fest-Verben.md) |
| *sicherstellen* | to ensure; to seize | *Die Polizei stellte Drogen sicher.* |
| *klarstellen* | to make clear | *Ich möchte eines klarstellen.* |
| *richtigstellen* | to correct, to set straight | *eine Behauptung richtigstellen* |
| *bereitstellen* | to provide, to allocate | *Mittel bereitstellen* |
| *wiederherstellen* | to restore | *die Ordnung wiederherstellen* |
| *zustellen* | to deliver (post) | *einen Brief zustellen* |
| *zurückstellen* | to defer, to postpone | *ein Projekt zurückstellen* |
| *gegenüberstellen* | to compare; to confront | *Zahlen gegenüberstellen* |
| *nachstellen* | to stalk; to re-enact | *jdm. nachstellen* |
| *entstellen* | to disfigure; to distort | *Tatsachen entstellen* |

## *sich stellen* — three distinct senses

| Construction | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *sich stellen* | to turn oneself in | *Der Täter stellte sich der Polizei.* |
| *sich* + dat. *stellen* | to face up to | *Er stellt sich der Verantwortung.* |
| *sich* + adj. *stellen* | to feign | *Er stellte sich schlafend / dumm.* |

## Set constructions (Funktionsverbgefüge)

A whole layer of formal German is built on *stellen* + noun. Learn them as
single units:

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *eine Frage stellen* | to ask a question |
| *einen Antrag stellen* | to file an application |
| *Bedingungen stellen* | to set conditions |
| *zur Verfügung stellen* | to make available |
| *infrage stellen* | to call into question |
| *unter Beweis stellen* | to demonstrate, to prove |
| *in Aussicht stellen* | to hold out the prospect of |
| *zur Diskussion stellen* | to open up for discussion |
| *in Rechnung stellen* | to charge for |
| *unter Strafe stellen* | to make punishable |
| *auf die Probe stellen* | to put to the test |

## How not to confuse them

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| *sich* **acc.** / *sich* **dat.** *vorstellen* | introduce oneself / imagine |
| *stellen* / *stehen* | to put (acc.) / to stand (dat.) |
| *herstellen* / *erstellen* | to make a thing / to draw up a document |
| *darstellen* / *vorstellen* | to depict / to introduce someone |
| *einstellen* / *anstellen* | to hire (formal) / to hire and to switch on |
| *bestellen* / *einstellen* | to order / to take on staff |
| *ÚMstellen* / *umSTÉLLEN* | to rearrange / to surround |

## Typical contexts

- **Work:** *jemanden einstellen*, *fest angestellt*, *sich vorstellen*,
  *eine Stelle suchen*.
- **Bureaucracy:** *einen Antrag stellen*, *ein Gutachten erstellen*,
  *Unterlagen zur Verfügung stellen*.
- **Everyday:** *Stell den Wecker!*, *das Auto abstellen*, *sich anstellen*.
- **Industry:** *hergestellt in Deutschland*, *die Produktion einstellen*.
- **News:** *Der Täter hat sich gestellt.*, *Die Polizei umstellte das
  Gebäude.*

## How to remember

The root is about **standing something upright**: an object, a person, a
question. The prefix says where:

- **vor** forward → **introduce** (put yourself forward) and **imagine**
  (put it before your own eyes);
- **ein** in → **hire** (into the staff), **adjust**, **discontinue**;
- **her** hither → **manufacture** (bring into being);
- **dar** forth, on show → **depict**;
- **aus** out → **exhibit**;
- **an** up against → **queue up**, **employ**;
- **ab** off → **set down**, **park**, **switch off**;
- **auf** up → **set up**, **nominate**;
- **um** around → **rearrange** or **surround**, by stress;
- **be-** covering the object → **order**, **appoint**.

One sentence with the core of the family: *Er hat **sich vorgestellt**,
wurde **eingestellt**, **stellt** jetzt Batterien **her** und **stellt**
alte Regeln **infrage**.* — “He introduced himself, was hired, now makes
batteries and calls the old rules into question.”
