# vernünftig — reasonable, sensible; 💬 proper, decent

One of the workhorse evaluative words of German. At the top of the
register it is Kant's **"in accordance with reason"**; at the bottom
it is everyday **"proper, decent, halfway good"** (*ein vernünftiger
Job*). It rates not a person's intelligence but the **soundness** of
a decision, an action or a thing.

## Grammar

| Feature | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Part of speech | adjective and adverb |
| Comparison | *vernünftig · vernünftiger · am vernünftigsten* |
| Attributive | *eine vernünftige Lösung* — a sensible solution |
| Predicative | *Das ist nicht vernünftig.* |
| Adverbial | *vernünftig essen / reden* — eat, talk properly |
| Negated | ***unvernünftig*** — unreasonable, foolhardy |
| Base noun | ***die Vernunft*** (singular only) — reason, sense |
| Also | *die Unvernunft* — folly; *vernunftbegabt* — rational |
| Government | ⚠️ none: *vernünftig sein*, never *vernünftig für* |

⚠️ **Don't confuse it with *verständlich*** "comprehensible" or
*verständnisvoll* "understanding, sympathetic". *Vernünftig* is
about **sound judgement**, not about being easy to follow.

## Main meanings

### 1. Sensible, reasonable (of people and actions)

> *Sei doch vernünftig!*
> Do be sensible!

> *Das war eine vernünftige Entscheidung.*
> That was a sensible decision.

> *Er ist alt genug, um vernünftig zu handeln.*
> He is old enough to act sensibly.

> *Vernünftige Menschen streiten sich nicht darüber.*
> Reasonable people don't quarrel about that.

### 2. Sound, well-founded (of grounds, doubts, prices)

> *Dafür gibt es keinen vernünftigen Grund.*
> There is no good reason for that.

> *Es bestehen keine vernünftigen Zweifel an seiner Schuld.*
> There is no reasonable doubt as to his guilt. (⚖️ a calque of the
> English *beyond reasonable doubt*)

> *Die Preise sind durchaus vernünftig.*
> The prices are perfectly reasonable.

### 3. 💬 Proper, decent, halfway good

The colloquial branch: "the way the thing ought to be", often with
a note of relief — *finally* a proper one.

> *Endlich mal ein vernünftiges Essen!*
> A proper meal at last!

> *Er sucht einen vernünftigen Job.*
> He's looking for a decent job.

> *Kauf dir mal ein vernünftiges Werkzeug.*
> Get yourself some proper tools for once.

> *Im Fernsehen kommt nichts Vernünftiges.*
> There's nothing worth watching on television.

### 4. Adverbially: properly, like a normal person

> *Rede vernünftig mit mir!*
> Talk to me properly!

> *Er kann nicht mal vernünftig kochen.*
> He can't even cook properly.

## Word breakdown and etymology

**ver-** + **nehm-** (→ *nunft*) + **-ig**

- **die Vernunft** comes from the verb **vernehmen** "to perceive,
  hear; ⚖️ to interrogate", itself **nehmen** "to take". The
  *nehm- / -nunft* alternation is the regular one: *ankommen →
  Ankunft*, *zusammenkommen → Zusammenkunft*.
- So **Vernunft** literally means **"the taking-in"**: the faculty
  that *takes* the world in and puts it in order.
- **-ig** is the quality suffix: "endowed with reason, in keeping
  with reason".

🔑 **Reason as "grasping" is a universal metaphor:**

| Language | Word | Literally |
|----------|------|-----------|
| German | *Vernunft* ← *vernehmen* ← *nehmen* | "taking" |
| German | *begreifen* ← *greifen* | "grasping" |
| English | *comprehend* ← Lat. *prehendere* | "seize" |
| English | *grasp*, *get it*, *catch on* | "take hold" |
| Latin | *intelligere* | "pick out among" |

German simply keeps the verb visible where English hides its
metaphor under Latin.

**The English cognates of *nehmen*** survive in odd corners: archaic
**to nim** "to take", **numb** (< *nimen* — feeling *taken* from
you), and **nimble** (OE *næmel* "quick to take hold"). So
*vernünftig* is a distant relative of *nimble*: both are about
quickness of grasp, one of the hands, the other of the head.

⚠️ **Vernunft vs. Verstand** — the classic German (and Kantian)
split, which English blurs into a single "reason":

| Word | Role | Kant |
|------|------|------|
| **der Verstand** | understanding: analyses, distinguishes | concepts |
| **die Vernunft** | reason: judges ends, principles, limits | ideas |

Colloquially: *Er hat viel Verstand, aber wenig Vernunft* — "plenty
of brains, no sense". Hence the title *Kritik der reinen Vernunft*
— *Critique of Pure Reason*, not of pure understanding.

## Distinctions from close words

| Word | Sense | Flavour |
|------|-------|---------|
| **vernünftig** | sensible; 💬 decent, proper | ✅ all-purpose |
| **verständig** | judicious, understanding | 📜 slightly bookish |
| **besonnen** | level-headed, prudent | 🎭 praise of character |
| **klug** | clever, smart | about ability |
| **weise** | wise | 🎭 elevated |
| **sinnvoll** | meaningful, worthwhile | of actions, not people |
| **anständig** | decent, respectable | ethics + everyday |
| **ordentlich** | proper, decent, thorough | 💬 close to sense 3 |
| **nüchtern** | sober, matter-of-fact | ⚠️ also "on an empty stomach" |

**Key pitfalls:**

- ⚠️ *vernünftig* ≠ *klug*. A clever person can do something daft;
  *vernünftig* judges the **choice**, not the intellect.
- ⚠️ *verständlich* is "comprehensible" (of speech or text),
  *verständnisvoll* is "sympathetic". Neither is *vernünftig*.
- ⚠️ *sinnvoll* is about whether a **measure makes sense**; of a
  person only *vernünftig* works.
- ⚠️ English *reasonable* leads exactly the same double life
  ("rational" + "acceptable, not expensive"), so here the literal
  translation is safe in both directions — a rare gift.

## Typical collocations

- *Sei vernünftig!* — be sensible!
- *zur Vernunft kommen* — to come to one's senses
- *jdn. zur Vernunft bringen* — to bring someone to their senses
- *aus vernünftigen Gründen* — for good reasons
- *eine vernünftige Lösung / Entscheidung / Erklärung*
- *vernünftige Preise* — reasonable prices
- *vernünftig essen / schlafen / leben* — to eat, sleep, live
  properly
- *nichts Vernünftiges* — nothing worthwhile
- *die Stimme der Vernunft* — the voice of reason
- *Vernunft annehmen* — 📜 to listen to reason

## How to remember

Read the word backwards: *vernünftig* ← *Vernunft* ← *vernehmen* ←
**nehmen**, "to take". **Reason is what you take the world in
with.** English hides the same picture inside *comprehend* ("seize")
and shows it plainly in *grasp* and *get it*. Two languages, one
gesture: to think is to take hold.

Keep the two storeys apart: **Verstand** does the calculating,
**Vernunft** decides whether the calculation is worth doing. That is
why Kant critiqued *pure reason* and not pure understanding.

And for daily life, remember the downhill sense: *ein vernünftiges
Essen* is not a philosophical dinner but **a proper meal** — just as
English *reasonable* came down from the heavens and started haggling
over prices.
