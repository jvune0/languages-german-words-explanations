# emp- (empfangen · empfehlen · empfinden · empören) — "towards, to meet"

> One family, one key: these four verbs are the complete list of native
> German words beginning with *emp-*. All of them hide the same prefix
> **ent-** in its oldest sense, "towards, to meet" — sound change simply
> turned *ent-f-* into *emp-f-*. The register is mostly neutral, with a
> formal wing (*empfangen*, *empören*).

## Grammar: what the whole group shares

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Prefix type | **inseparable** (*untrennbar*), never stressed |
| Stress | on the root: *empFANgen*, *empFEHlen*, *empFINden* |
| Past participle | **no *ge-***: *empfangen*, *empfohlen*, *empfunden* |
| *zu*-infinitive | *zu empfangen* (never *empzufangen*) |
| Conjugation | inherited from the root verb: *fangen*, *finden* |
| Auxiliary | always *haben* |
| Size of group | four verbs — that is the entire native list |

### Principal parts

| Verb | Present (3sg) | Preterite | Participle II |
|---|---|---|---|
| **empfangen** | *empfängt* | *empfing* | *hat empfangen* |
| **empfehlen** | *empfiehlt* | *empfahl* | *hat empfohlen* |
| **empfinden** | *empfindet* | *empfand* | *hat empfunden* |
| **(sich) empören** | *empört* | *empörte* | *hat empört* |

The first three are strong and simply echo their parent verbs: *fangen
→ fing → gefangen*, *finden → fand → gefunden*, *stehlen → stahl →
gestohlen*. The fourth is weak, being built on an adverb, not a verb.

## The key to the whole family: *emp-* = *ent-* = "towards"

The prefix **ent-** goes back to Germanic ***and-*** "against,
towards" — the very element in **Ant**wort (= English **answer**, "a
word given back") and in Greek *anti-*. In modern German *ent-*
usually means "un-, de-, away" (*entfernen* to remove, *entfalten* to
unfold), but in this quartet the **archaic sense survives**:

> **emp- + root = "to take in what comes towards you"**

Why did *ent-* become *emp-*? Two sound changes in a row:

1. **Assimilation**: the dental *n* before the labial *f* moves to the
   lips and becomes *m* (*ntf → mtf*).
2. **Epenthesis**: between nasal *m* and fricative *f* a transitional
   stop *p* slips in (*mf → mpf*).

English shows the same epenthesis in *empty* (Old English *ǣmettig*),
*Thompson* (Thom + son) and *Hampshire* (Hamtun + shire). German
generalised it inside roots too: *Sumpf* ↔ English *swamp*, *Dampf* ↔
*damp*.

**Why only four words?** The change froze only where speakers stopped
hearing a prefix at all — nobody parses *empfehlen*. Wherever the
prefix stays transparent, German spells it out: *ent**f**ernen*,
*ent**f**alten*, *ent**f**liehen*, *ent**f**ühren*.

⚠️ **False relatives.** *Empirie*, *empirisch*, *Emphase*, *Empathie*
have nothing to do with this family: they carry Greek *en-/em-* "in"
(+ *peira* "trial", *pathos* "feeling"). Same letters, other ancestry.

## The verbs

### 1. *empfangen* — to receive, to welcome

Government: *jdn./etw. empfangen*. The core is "to catch (*fangen*)
what comes towards you" — hence both the ceremonial welcome and radio
reception.

> *Der Präsident empfing die Delegation im Schloss Bellevue.*
> The President received the delegation at Bellevue Palace.

> *Hier empfange ich kein Signal.*
> I get no signal here.

> *Sie wurde mit Applaus empfangen.*
> She was greeted with applause.

In biblical and medical language it means "to conceive": *sie empfing
ein Kind*, and *die Unbefleckte Empfängnis* is the Immaculate
Conception.

⚠️ Register: *empfangen* is loftier than plain *bekommen*. You
*empfängst* guests and delegations; a parcel you simply *bekommst*.

### 2. *empfehlen* — to recommend

Government: *jdm. etw. empfehlen*; *jdm. empfehlen, etw. zu tun*. The
impersonal *es empfiehlt sich, …* means "it is advisable to …".

> *Ich kann Ihnen dieses Restaurant wärmstens empfehlen.*
> I can warmly recommend this restaurant to you.

> *Es empfiehlt sich, früh zu buchen.*
> It is advisable to book early.

> *Er hat mich für die Stelle empfohlen.*
> He recommended me for the position.

Reflexive *sich empfehlen* in its old-fashioned sense is "to take
one's leave": *Ich empfehle mich!* — the exact tone of English "I shall
take my leave" (📜 dated).

**Note:** the root is **not** modern *fehlen* "to be missing" (a loan
from Old French, cf. English *fail*), but ancient Germanic
***felhan*** "to entrust, conceal" — the same root inside *befehlen*
"to command". So *empfehlen* is literally "to entrust someone towards
someone else".

### 3. *empfinden* — to feel, to perceive inwardly

Government: *etw.* (acc.) *empfinden*; very common is *etw. als etw.
empfinden* — "to perceive sth. as sth.".

> *Ich empfand große Erleichterung.*
> I felt great relief.

> *Er empfand die Bemerkung als Beleidigung.*
> He took the remark as an insult.

> *Sie empfindet nichts mehr für ihn.*
> She no longer feels anything for him.

The core is "to find (*finden*) within oneself": a sensation is
imagined as a discovery. *Empfinden* is always a shade more inward and
more literary than *fühlen*. For the adjective, see the card on
*empfindlich*.

### 4. *(sich) empören* — to outrage, to be outraged

Government: *sich über etw.* (acc.) *empören*; transitively *etw.
empört mich*. The participle *empört* "outraged" and the adjective
*empörend* "outrageous" are even commoner than the verb itself.

> *Die Öffentlichkeit empörte sich über das Urteil.*
> The public was outraged at the verdict.

> *Das ist ja empörend!*
> That's outrageous!

> *Er reagierte empört auf den Vorwurf.*
> He reacted indignantly to the accusation.

Here *emp-* stands before **b**, not *f*, but the mechanism is
identical: *ent + bor* → *empor* "upwards" (cf. *die Empore*, a gallery
in a church). So *sich empören* is "to rise up against" → to revolt →
to be indignant. English has the same image in *uprising* and *up in
arms*: anger goes upwards in both languages.

## How not to confuse them

### *empfehlen* vs. *befehlen*

One root, *\*felhan* "to entrust", two prefixes — and two degrees of
force:

| Verb | Forms | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **empfehlen** | *empfiehlt · empfahl · empfohlen* | to recommend |
| **befehlen** | *befiehlt · befahl · befohlen* | to command |

The forms differ by **one letter in the prefix** — a classic trap in
listening. The logic: *be-* makes the act binding (an order), *emp-*
keeps it a gesture offered towards you (advice).

### *empfangen* vs. its neighbours

| Verb | Core idea | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **empfangen** | to receive, welcome (ceremonially) | *Gäste empfangen* |
| **bekommen** | to get (neutral, everyday) | *ein Paket bekommen* |
| **erhalten** | to receive (formal, written) | *eine Absage erhalten* |
| **annehmen** | to accept, agree to take | *ein Geschenk annehmen* |
| **abholen** | to meet = to pick up | *jdn. am Bahnhof abholen* |

### *empfinden* vs. its neighbours

| Verb | Core idea |
|---|---|
| **empfinden** | to feel inwardly, more literary (*Reue empfinden*) |
| **fühlen** | to feel — the general, everyday word |
| **spüren** | to sense physically, notice in the body (*Kälte*) |
| **merken** | to notice, to realise (*Ich merke es*) |
| **wahrnehmen** | to perceive (neutral, near-technical) |

### *sich empören* vs. its neighbours

| Verb | Core idea |
|---|---|
| **sich empören** | to be outraged (moral, public) |
| **sich aufregen** | to get worked up (everyday) |
| **sich ärgern** | to be annoyed (personal) |
| **sich entrüsten** | to be indignant (📜 bookish near-synonym) |

## Register and style

| Setting | Who from the family lives there |
|---|---|
| 💬 Conversation | *empfehlen*; *sich aufregen* replaces *empören* |
| 📰 Press | *Empörung*, *empört*, *empfangen* (state visits) |
| 💼 Business writing | *empfehlen*, *Empfehlung*, *Empfänger* |
| 📡 Technology | *empfangen*, *Empfänger*, *Empfang* |
| 📚 Literature | *empfinden*, *Empfindung*, *empfindsam* |
| ⛪ Church | *empfangen* (Sakramente), *Empfängnis* |
| 📜 Dated | *Ich empfehle mich* — "I take my leave" |

## Typical collocations

- *Gäste / eine Delegation / einen Preis empfangen*
- *keinen Empfang haben* — to have no signal, no reception
- *am Empfang fragen* — to ask at the front desk
- *jdm. ein Buch wärmstens empfehlen* — to recommend warmly
- *es empfiehlt sich, … zu tun* — it is advisable to …
- *Freude / Schmerz / Reue empfinden* — to feel joy, pain, remorse
- *etw. als ungerecht empfinden* — to perceive sth. as unjust
- *sich über etw. empören* — to be outraged at sth.
- *helle Empörung auslösen* — to provoke sheer outrage

## How to remember

One scene holds all four: **the host *empfängt* the guest (catches him
coming towards the door), *empfiehlt* him the wine (entrusts advice
towards him), the guest *empfindet* pleasure (finds it inside himself)
— and on seeing the bill *empört sich* and rises from the table.**

Everything in *emp-* moves **towards** you: a welcome, a piece of
advice, a feeling, a protest. And remember the sound recipe: *ent + f*
— the tongue drifts to the lips (*m*) and trips over an inserted *p*.
